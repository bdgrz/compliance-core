## Identity, invariant and boundary

One tenant/program/rollover stream owns a reviewed carry-forward proposal and independent approval. The selected invariant in `aggregates.json` and `PROGRAM_ROLLOVER_EXECUTION` in `decisions.json` requires one atomic claim of the exact approved version and digest before successor work, so a concurrent revision cannot redirect work already in progress. The claimed version freezes exact next examination, plan, period and dates. Keep claim and version on this owner; a separate private durable process coordinates existing examination, Type I baseline and outcome, system-description, Type II plan and period owners. It cannot use a projection to win the claim or manufacture those owners' facts. Handler, process, reactor and API implementation are later stages and were not added here.

## Current implementation and executable coverage

`ProgramRollover.Propose` validates a completed Type II period, exact close and audit outcome and normalized carry-forward content; `Approve` enforces independent personal approval; `Revise` permits a changed proposal while status is approved until `period_created`. `RecordNextPeriodCreated` emits `ProgramNextPeriodCreated` after matching planned IDs and timestamp but does not itself prove target-period completion. `ProgramRolloverTests` exercise valid completed sources, no historical-occurrence carry-forward, independent approval, exact proposal/approval retry and valid replay. Those tests do not establish a revise-versus-execution fence, claim retry or actual target completion; they were not rerun in this phase.
