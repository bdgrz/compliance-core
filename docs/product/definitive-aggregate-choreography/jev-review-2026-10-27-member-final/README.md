# Member aggregate review

A Member is identified by the existing tenant/user UUIDv5 identity. Its current affiliation, expiry, suspension and membership episode belong to one owner. Deprovisioning ends the current episode; a new episode requires the exact cleanup completion. Team and grant cleanup remains outside the member, and its completion is the member's admission gate. Portia supplies stream hydration and persistence.

The existing registration command guard enforced the episode gate, but replay accepted a second live registration or a registration before cleanup. Registration replay also did not verify its tenant, member and user against the owner; suspension and reinstatement replay did not verify those fact identities either. The aggregate now rejects these invalid facts before applying their state. This deliberately makes malformed histories fail hydration; existing persisted histories have not been audited for such facts.

TDD evidence: `ShouldRejectAnotherMembershipEpisodeGivenLiveOrUncleanedEpisodeOnReplay` failed for both cases before the lifecycle guard; `ShouldRejectRegistrationGivenDifferentMemberIdentityOnReplay` failed for all three identity mismatches before the registration identity guard; `ShouldRejectLifecycleFactGivenDifferentMemberIdentityOnReplay` failed for all six suspension/reinstatement mismatches before the lifecycle identity guard. The focused membership lifecycle, retirement recovery, deprovision handler and transition transport selection then passed 61 tests. It includes successful fresh registration after cleanup and recovery of prior membership cleanup.

Focused judgments supported the episode gate (0.96), registration identity (0.97), suspension/reinstatement identity (0.97), successful successor after cleanup (0.96) and the specified replay tests (0.92).
