# Aggregate boundary and migration ledger

Status: **historical baseline; use the current [2026-10-23 aggregate and reactor specification](audits/2026-10-23/README.md) for the 103-owner inventory and current target boundaries.** The rows below retain their original 102-owner snapshot. This ledger supports [#1022](https://github.com/bdgrz/compliance-core/issues/1022) and joins each currently inventoried aggregate to its source identity and stated invariant. The source-linked fact, reducer, producer, request, and handler map remains [aggregate-command-coverage.json](aggregate-command-coverage.json); it is not proof that a stream boundary is right or bounded.

## Acceptance rules

- Keep one aggregate per identity whose invariants require atomic decisions. Do not combine independently changing findings, evaluations, risks, periods, campaigns, requests, or other records into a broad program/tenant stream without an accepted finite domain bound.
- Record concrete finite bounds for every retained collection and serialized event payload. A field maximum alone does not prove a combined event fits Fitz's frame. Do not invent arbitrary business caps to avoid a split.
- Where identity changes, migrate one source fact at a time through authorized event-store writes, preserve source event ID/version/time/type/payload lineage, resume safely, and verify exact per-source/per-target parity before switching writers or readers. Use fresh projection identities for new event areas. Never mutate the event-store database directly.
- Drain or fence old writers before cutover. After all required histories and consumers have a verified target, remove legacy producers, aggregate reducers, active readers, and compatibility write paths. If append-only source records still use a legacy wire type, keep only the decoder or upcaster needed to read that retained history; remove it when a supported archive path exists or no retained record references it. Keep policy-gated and superseded facts classified; do not invent behavior from dormant contracts.
