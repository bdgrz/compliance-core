## Wiring boundary and smallest next capability

Existing DI/API exposes intake, inspection recording/release, redaction preparation/approval/read, request lifecycle, support linking and direct downloads. Target classification mutation, redaction rejection, support withdrawal, automatic disclosure and artifact disposal are absent.

Smallest independent candidate: EV-05 support withdrawal, including active/as-of consumer semantics. It uses an established exact-source owner and does not depend on selecting a scanner, redesigning request target terms, or introducing Retention disposition authority.

No context-wide completion or newly executed validation is claimed.
