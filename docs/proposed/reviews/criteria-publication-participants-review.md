# Criteria publication participants review

Status: **provisional implementation; not enabled or qualified**. Each publication start, witness pin, witness refusal, edition abort and witness fence creates an independent, deterministic, source-verified signal. The shared source checkpoint acknowledges only a matching durable process record. `ProcessSweep` resumes each signal independently; a pending witness cannot block unrelated signals at the source checkpoint.

An entry owner may refuse a selected publication attempt only at an exact target position with no pending mutation, no live pin, and no matching accepted criterion incarnation. The refusal captures the committed publication-start identity and fences a late pin. A durable refusal signal drives the exact edition abort; the abort signal fences the witness; the target fence signal completes edition cleanup. Positive source facts and attempt identities are verified on each hop. Unknown outcomes remain pending.

A publication requires the exact committed start and pin in source order, the original label request/reservation/edition-decision/confirmation chain, a live witness pin and label claim, and qualified witness, label and edition heads. The edition append uses the certified expected edition position and must be observed as a committed `CriteriaEditionPublished` fact before success. That fact is the structural publication cut. No criteria roster is read.

The cross-owner argument also relies on release and fence handlers accepting only exact committed edition terminal facts, and on edition terminal transitions competing with publication on the same edition position. These properties, the actual Fitz/Portia freshness and conditional-append behavior, unknown-commit recovery, and process-record durability are not qualified. `ICriteriaFreshEntryHead`, `ICriteriaFreshLabelClaimHead`, and `ICriteriaFreshEditionHead` have no granting production provider. Do not enable the process or treat it as feature-complete until those guarantees and race tests pass. The existing synchronous `CriteriaPublicationWitnessReactor` must stay disabled; its shared checkpoint can stall on unresolved work.

## Cross-owner race traces

Let the committed `CriteriaPublicationStarted` be edition resource position `p`, and let its matching committed `CriterionWitnessPinned` be witness resource position `w`. The publication handler observes the live pin and a qualified edition head `e` (where `e` is at least `p + 1`), then attempts the edition publication at expected position `e`. The only authorized target fence names an already committed `CriteriaPublicationAborted` for the same edition, draft generation, attempt, ordinal, identifier and incarnation. To race publication from the same edition head, abort must append at `e` too. If abort wins `e`, the target fence can later append to the witness stream, but the publication append at `e` must fail. If publication wins `e`, the abort append at `e` must fail, and no matching committed abort exists to authorize that fence. If another edition event wins first, the loser must re-observe and re-certify; it must not rebase a stale decision. This argument depends on effective exact-position CAS, source-authenticated abort-to-fence routing, and the absence of any other authorized route that clears a live pin.

For label text `L`, the start names an exact edition, label operation, generation `g` and confirmation. The publisher observes that exact claim as live and certified before attempting edition position `e`. A legitimate release of that exact claim needs a committed edition cancellation/refusal of that request, abandonment of `L/g`, or completed rename away from `L/g`; these are edition terminal decisions. A terminal decision that races publication from edition position `e` must compete with the publication append at `e`. A terminal decision already committed before the start cannot leave that same operation and generation as the edition's current confirmed label: cancellation/refusal cannot complete that request, abandonment ends the draft, and completed rename replaces the current label. A later return to the same text uses a different operation and a new claim generation. A delayed release of `L/g` matches only the old selected reservation and cannot release the later holder. This reasoning depends on **every** release and fence route validating the exact committed edition terminal source and exact claim generation, the edition owner enforcing its lifecycle, and the claim store enforcing target CAS. Those routes and the real provider must be audited and race-tested before registration; the present implementation does not prove them.

`CriteriaPublicationRaceTests` exercises both orderings of the edition abort/publication CAS, abandonment competing with publication start, and an old-generation release retry after another holder acquires the label, using the in-memory event store. These tests isolate owner and source-position behavior; they do not qualify Fitz, the actual process driver, release-route coverage, or end-to-end concurrent delivery, and they have not been run.
