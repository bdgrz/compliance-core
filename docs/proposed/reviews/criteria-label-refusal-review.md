# Criteria label refusal review

Status: **implemented provisionally; not qualified or enabled**. The per-label owner records a refusal only when its conditional decision observes a different live reservation. The refusal binds the claimant's edition, operation, requested generation, original request and actor to the other holder's exact reservation and generation. Its selected-operation replay retains only one refusal, refuses changed intent and prevents a later reservation of that operation. The label stream itself is still unbounded in lifetime.

The committer requires `ICriteriaFreshLabelClaimHead` to certify the exact resource head and compare-and-swap lineage; no granting provider is registered. It compares the streamed and hydrated participant state, uses the captured expected position, verifies the committed refusal against the claim and edition streams, and reports ambiguous outcomes as pending. An exact retry reuses the refusal's original requested generation even after later holders change the label generation. The signal worker propagates only source-verified refusals and remains unregistered until the process store, fresh-head, and append behavior are qualified.

Serialization and registration remain coordinator work: `EditionLabelReservationRefused`, `DecideCriteriaEditionLabelRefusal`, and `ApplyCriteriaEditionLabelRefusal` must be included in the relevant event/request contexts, and the signal reactor, process definition, refusal committer and receiver handlers must be integrated together. A provider that cannot certify currentness and effective conditional appends must not return a positive head certificate.

Jev `jev-1.14.0` returned 0.95 that the decision requires a different live holder, 0.77 that changed request intent is rejected, and 0.03 that production storage guarantees are already qualified. Its first broad exact-retry judgment was inconclusive (0.41). A narrower review of the actual replay branch and specified selected state returned 0.96 for exact retry success, 0.94 for rejecting a rebased generation, and 0.12 for appending another event. These are review signals, not proof. The preserved requests and responses are `criteria-label-refusal-jev-{request,response}.json` and `criteria-label-refusal-retry-jev-{request,response}.json`.

Focused aggregate, source-verifier, signal and committer tests were authored, including changed intent, replay after holder change, lost acknowledgement and release winning a race. Compilation and tests were deferred for the feature chunk; none have been run.
